About This Flight

Welcome to FlyToLearn Basic! This introductory challenge has you fly a short cross-country hop from Boeing Field (KBFI) to Renton Municipal Airport (KRNT) — just 3 nautical miles southeast in the Seattle area using X-Plane 12's built-in demo scenery. No add-on scenery or registration required.

The plugin scores your flight automatically based on distance efficiency, fuel burned, flight time, and landing quality. Your goal is to fly a direct, smooth flight and land on the correct runway. The score screen appears as soon as you come to a full stop.

Step-by-Step

  • Load X-Plane 12 and spawn at KBFI — Boeing Field. Confirm the FlyToLearn logo bar is visible at the bottom of the screen.
  • Click the FlyToLearn logo to open the start screen. Confirm the route shows KBFI → KRNT, then click Start.
  • Take off from Runway 13L or 13R, heading southeast (~130°). Once airborne, the plugin begins recording your flight.
  • Fly directly southeast toward KRNT — only ~3 nm away. No need for a full traffic pattern unless you want the practice.
  • Set up a straight-in approach to land on Runway 16, heading south (~160°).
    Approach from the NORTH: KBFI is north of KRNT, so flying straight ahead puts you on Runway 16. If you overshoot and loop back from the south you will land on Runway 34 (wrong direction) and be disqualified.
  • Touch down near the north threshold of Runway 16. Land early — do not float past the midpoint before touching down.
  • Slow to a full stop. The score screen appears automatically.

Tips for a Good Score

Fly a direct route A straight line from KBFI to KRNT gives the best distance/time ratio. Avoid unnecessary detours or circuits.
Land smoothly A gentle touchdown (low G-force) avoids the 5% hard-landing penalty. Aim for a steady, controlled descent.
Conserve fuel Less fuel burned improves your score. Fly at a reasonable cruise power setting — this is a short hop, so there's no need for full power the whole way.
Sim speed is locked at 1× The plugin prevents time acceleration during the flight. You cannot fast-forward.
