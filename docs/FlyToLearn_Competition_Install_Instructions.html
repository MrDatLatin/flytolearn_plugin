What Is FlyToLearn Competition?

FlyToLearn Competition is an advanced X-Plane 12 plugin that scores your training flights through the French Alps. It records distance, fuel burn, flight time, and landing quality, then displays a scored result when you stop. This is the competition-grade version of FlyToLearn, designed for the GAMA Challenge route.

Competition route: LFHU → LFLJ Altiport Huez (Alpe d'Huez) to Courchevel Altiport — approximately 25 nautical miles through high Alpine terrain. You must land on Runway 04 at Courchevel. This is the only permitted landing direction.
This guide has three parts — complete all three in order. 1. Authenticate X-Plane 12 with your XDD product key  →  2. Download the Western Europe scenery  →  3. Install the FlyToLearn Competition plugin.

Can both plugins coexist? Yes. FlyToLearn Basic and FlyToLearn Competition can both be installed at the same time — they run independently without conflict.

Step 1 — Launch X-Plane 12 & Authenticate

X-Plane 12 is pre-installed on your school computer. The first time you launch it, X-Plane may ask how you want to authenticate. Use the steps below to ensure you select the correct option.

Important: Choose "I have an XDD or DVD" — not X-Plane Identity Your school uses X-Plane Digital Download (XDD) licensing. Do not create or sign in with a personal X-Plane Identity account. Your instructor will provide the product key.
  • Launch X-Plane 12. If this is the first time on this computer, an authentication screen will appear.
  • Select "I have an XDD or DVD" (X-Plane Digital Download). Do not click "X-Plane Identity" or sign in with an online account.
  • Enter the product key provided by your instructor when prompted, then click Continue.
  • X-Plane 12 will validate the key and proceed. Continue to Step 2 to download the required scenery.
Already authenticated? If X-Plane 12 opens directly to the main screen without asking for a key, your computer is already set up correctly. Skip ahead to Step 2.

Step 2 — Download Western Europe Scenery

The competition route flies through the French Alps. X-Plane 12 includes a built-in scenery installer — you will use it to download the Western Europe region, which includes both LFHU (Alpe d'Huez) and LFLJ (Courchevel).

Ask your instructor for help with this step if you are unsure. The scenery download requires the same XDD product key used in Step 1. Do not sign in with X-Plane Identity.
  • From the X-Plane 12 main screen, navigate to the airport selector and search for LFHU or LFLJ.
  • If the scenery for this region has not been downloaded yet, X-Plane will display a prompt offering to install it. Click to proceed.
  • If X-Plane asks you to authenticate again, select "I have an XDD or DVD" and enter your product key. Do not use X-Plane Identity.
  • When prompted to choose a scenery region, select Western Europe. This region includes France and the Alps airports used in this course.
  • Allow the download to complete. Depending on your internet connection this may take several minutes. Do not close X-Plane during the download.
  • Once the download finishes, X-Plane will confirm the scenery is ready. You can now proceed to Step 3.

Step 3 — Install the Plugin

If you have never installed FlyToLearn Competition before, follow these steps.

  • Unzip FlyToLearn_Competition_v1.4.2.zip. You will see a folder named FlyToLearn_Competition.
  • Copy the FlyToLearn_Competition folder into your X-Plane 12 plugins directory:
    X-Plane 12/
      Resources/
        plugins/
          FlyToLearn_Competition/  ← place the folder here
            64/   liblinux/
            data/modules/
              main.lua   Custom Module/
    Folder must go directly inside plugins/ — not inside a subfolder. On Mac the full path is: ~/X-Plane 12/Resources/plugins/FlyToLearn_Competition/
    On Windows: C:\X-Plane 12\Resources\plugins\FlyToLearn_Competition\
  • Launch X-Plane 12. The FlyToLearn logo bar appears at the bottom of the screen when the plugin loads successfully.
  • Spawn at LFHU (Altiport Huez) to begin. See the separate flight instruction sheet for step-by-step flying guidance, including the Courchevel one-way runway rules.

Updating from a Previous Version

If you have an older FlyToLearn plugin already installed, remove it completely before installing the new version.

Delete the old folder before installing — do not overwrite. Overwriting can leave behind outdated configuration files that cause scoring errors such as wrong-runway disqualifications or score = 0.
  • Quit X-Plane 12 completely.
  • Find and delete any existing FlyToLearn plugin folder from X-Plane 12/Resources/plugins/:
    X-Plane 12/Resources/plugins/
      FlyToLearn/  ← delete if present
      FlyToLearn_Competition/  ← delete if present
    Deleting the folder also removes any stored config file inside it — no separate config deletion needed.
  • Follow Step 3 — Install the Plugin above to install v1.4.2 fresh.
Note on settings: Your previous scoring weights and preferences reset to defaults when the old folder is deleted. This is intentional — it ensures the 2-minute minimum flight time and all other parameters are correct for v1.4.2.

After Launch — What to Expect

Logo bar at the bottom The FlyToLearn logo appears as a thin bar at the bottom of the X-Plane window. Click it to open the start panel.
Start panel shows the route The panel will display LFHU → LFLJ. Click Start when you are ready on the runway at LFHU.
Score screen on landing After coming to a full stop at LFLJ, the score screen appears automatically with your result and landing quality grade.
Sim speed locked at 1× The plugin disables time acceleration during the scored flight. Plan for the full ~25 nm transit at real-time pace.

Troubleshooting

ProblemFix
Logo bar not visible after loading Check Plugins → FlyToLearn Competition → Show FlyToLearn Competition in the X-Plane menu bar to toggle the window on
Score is always zero or "DISQUALIFIED" Delete the old plugin folder completely and reinstall fresh. An old flytolearn_config.ini with incorrect settings is the most common cause.
"Wrong runway" after landing on Rwy 04 You must approach from the southwest and touch down near the bottom (south) threshold. If you overshot the midpoint before landing, the plugin detects it as a Rwy 22 landing. Go around and re-establish from the southwest.
No score appears after stopping You must be airborne for at least 2 minutes before landing counts. Fly the full LFHU → LFLJ route without landing early.
Courchevel airport looks basic or is missing Western Europe scenery may not be installed. Return to Step 2 and use X-Plane's built-in scenery installer to download the Western Europe region. Ask your instructor for help.
Plugin not loading at all Confirm the FlyToLearn_Competition folder sits directly inside Resources/plugins/ — not nested inside another subfolder. Check X-Plane 12/Log.txt for plugin errors.
X-Plane asks me to sign in or authenticate Select "I have an XDD or DVD" and enter the product key provided by your instructor. Do not create a personal X-Plane Identity account.
