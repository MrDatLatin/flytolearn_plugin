About This Flight

Welcome to FlyToLearn Competition! This advanced challenge takes you through the French Alps from Altiport Huez (LFHU), high above Alpe d'Huez, to the legendary Courchevel Altiport (LFLJ) — one of the most demanding airports in the world. The flight covers approximately 25 nautical miles across high Alpine terrain.

The plugin scores your flight on distance efficiency, fuel burned, flight time, and landing quality. Landing precision is critical here: Courchevel has a short, steeply sloped runway with strictly one-way operations. The score screen appears as soon as you come to a full stop.

⚠ Courchevel One-Way Operations — Read Before Flying
Courchevel Altiport operates one direction at a time due to its extreme 18.5% uphill gradient. These rules apply in real operations and are enforced by the plugin.
✈ LANDING — Runway 04 only Approach from the southwest. Land heading northeast (~044°), touching down at the lower (south) end of the runway. The uphill slope helps you stop.
✈ TAKEOFF — Runway 22 only Depart heading southwest (~224°), rolling downhill off the upper end. You are arriving, not departing — do not attempt to land from this direction.

Step-by-Step

  • Load X-Plane 12 and spawn at LFHU — Altiport Huez. Confirm the FlyToLearn logo bar is visible at the bottom of the screen.
  • Click the FlyToLearn logo to open the start screen. Confirm the route shows LFHU → LFLJ, then click Start.
  • Take off from LFHU and climb to a safe altitude. Surrounding Alpine terrain is high — do not descend until you are established on your approach to Courchevel.
  • Navigate approximately 25 nm northeast through the French Alps toward LFLJ. Plan your fuel and descent early — there is little room to maneuver near Courchevel.
  • Set up your approach to arrive from the southwest, lining up to land on Runway 04 heading northeast (~044°).
    Critical — approach from the SOUTHWEST only: You must be established southwest of the field and fly inbound (northeast) to land on Runway 04. If you approach from the northeast, you will land heading southwest on Runway 22 — this is prohibited and will result in immediate disqualification. If you overshoot, execute a go-around and re-establish from the southwest.
  • Touch down at the lower (south) end of Runway 04 — the very bottom of the slope. Land early and on-speed.
    Do not land long: Courchevel's runway is only 537 m (1,762 ft) and slopes steeply uphill. Floating past the midpoint leaves insufficient runway to stop and will result in disqualification.
  • The uphill slope will help slow the aircraft. Slow to a full stop — the score screen appears automatically.

Tips for a Good Score

Plan your descent early Courchevel sits at 6,588 ft. Begin descending well before the field and arrive established on final from the southwest. Last-minute maneuvering in Alpine terrain is dangerous.
Fly on-speed — not too fast Arriving too fast makes it impossible to stop on a 537 m runway. Stabilize your approach speed early and resist the urge to carry extra knots "for safety."
Touch down at the threshold Land at the very bottom of the slope (the south/lower end of Rwy 04). Every metre of runway counts. A firm, early touchdown beats a smooth float every time.
Sim speed is locked at 1× The plugin prevents time acceleration during the flight. Plan the full ~25 nm transit at real-time pace.
