What Is FlyToLearn Basic?

FlyToLearn Basic is an X-Plane 12 plugin that scores your training flights automatically. It records your distance, fuel burn, flight time, and landing quality, then displays a score when you come to a full stop. No special scenery is required — the Basic route uses X-Plane 12's built-in default scenery.

Training route: KBFI → KRNT Boeing Field (Seattle) to Renton Municipal Airport — approximately 3 nautical miles. A short, accessible intro flight for new students.

First-Time X-Plane 12 Launch — Licensing

X-Plane 12 is pre-installed on your school computer. The first time you launch it, X-Plane may ask how you want to authenticate. Use the steps below to ensure you select the correct option.

Important: Choose "I have an XDD or DVD" — not X-Plane Identity Your school uses X-Plane Digital Download (XDD) licensing. Do not create or sign in with a personal X-Plane Identity account. Your instructor will provide the product key if needed.
  • Launch X-Plane 12. If this is the first time on this computer, an authentication screen will appear.
  • Select "I have an XDD or DVD" (X-Plane Digital Download). Do not click "X-Plane Identity" or sign in with an online account.
  • Enter the product key provided by your instructor when prompted, then click Continue.
  • X-Plane 12 will validate the key and proceed to the main screen. You are now ready to install the FlyToLearn plugin.
Already authenticated? If X-Plane 12 opens directly to the main screen without asking for a key, your computer is already set up correctly. Skip to the Fresh Install section below.

Fresh Install — New Students

If you have never installed FlyToLearn before, follow these steps.

  • Unzip FlyToLearn_Basic_v1.3.2.zip. You will see a folder named FlyToLearn_Basic.
  • Copy the FlyToLearn_Basic folder into your X-Plane 12 plugins directory:
    X-Plane 12/
      Resources/
        plugins/
          FlyToLearn_Basic/  ← place the folder here
            64/   liblinux/
            data/modules/
              main.lua   Custom Module/
    Folder must go directly inside plugins/ — not inside a subfolder. On Mac the full path is: ~/X-Plane 12/Resources/plugins/FlyToLearn_Basic/
    On Windows: C:\X-Plane 12\Resources\plugins\FlyToLearn_Basic\
  • Launch X-Plane 12. The FlyToLearn logo bar will appear at the bottom of the screen once the plugin loads successfully.
  • Spawn at KBFI (Boeing Field) to begin your first flight. See the separate flight instruction sheet for step-by-step flying guidance.

Updating from a Previous Version

If you have an older FlyToLearn plugin already installed, remove it completely before installing the new version.

Delete the old folder before installing — do not overwrite. Overwriting can leave behind outdated files. A clean install prevents scoring errors.
  • Quit X-Plane 12 completely.
  • Find and delete the old plugin folder from X-Plane 12/Resources/plugins/. It may be named FlyToLearn, FlyToLearn_Basic, or similar:
    X-Plane 12/Resources/plugins/
      FlyToLearn/  ← delete if present
      FlyToLearn_Basic/  ← delete if present
  • Follow the Fresh Install steps above to install v1.3.2.
Note on settings: Your previous scoring weights and preferences will reset to defaults when the old folder is deleted. This is intentional — it ensures the minimum flight time and other parameters are correct for v1.3.2.

After Launch — What to Expect

Logo bar at the bottom The FlyToLearn logo appears as a thin bar along the bottom edge of the X-Plane window. Click it to open the start panel.
Start panel shows the route The start panel will display KBFI → KRNT. Click Start when you are ready on the runway at KBFI.
Score screen on landing After coming to a full stop at KRNT, the score screen appears automatically with your result and landing quality.
Sim speed locked at 1× The plugin disables time acceleration during the scored flight. You cannot fast-forward.

Troubleshooting

ProblemFix
Logo bar not visible after loading Check Plugins → FlyToLearn Basic → Show FlyToLearn Basic in the X-Plane menu bar to toggle the window on
Score is always zero or "DISQUALIFIED" Delete the old FlyToLearn plugin folder completely, then reinstall fresh per the steps above
Start panel shows wrong route Make sure you are using the FlyToLearn_Basic folder, not an older FlyToLearn install. Both can coexist but must be separate folders.
No score appears after stopping You must be airborne for at least 2 minutes before landing counts. Fly the full route from KBFI to KRNT.
Plugin not loading at all Confirm the FlyToLearn_Basic folder sits directly inside Resources/plugins/ — not nested inside another subfolder. Check X-Plane 12/Log.txt for plugin errors.
X-Plane asks me to sign in or authenticate Select "I have an XDD or DVD" and enter the product key provided by your instructor. Do not create a personal X-Plane Identity account.
