----------------------------------------------------------
FlyToLearn Competition 1.4.4 — 2026-04-26
----------------------------------------------------------
- Fixed: "Landed on Wrong Runway" disqualification when touchdown
  was actually inside the safe zone. Touchdown position is now
  recorded at the actual moment of wheel contact, not at the
  point where the plane slowed to ~45 kts.
- Fixed: False "Enroute" banner during takeoff roll at ~45 kts.
  Phase transition now uses gear-height (sim/flightmodel2/gear/y_agl)
  instead of the unreliable onground_all weight-on-wheels sensor.
- Added: Per-gear height pre-capture using vh_ind_fpm2
  (instantaneous vertical speed) for accurate landing scoring.
