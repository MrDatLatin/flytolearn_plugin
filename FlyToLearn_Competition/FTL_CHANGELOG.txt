FlyToLearn Competition 1.4.3 — 2026-11-12
----------------------------------------------------------
- Added: Takeoff minimum-speed gate (TAKEOFF_MIN_SPEED_MS = 20 m/s,
  ~39 knots). DEPARTING -> INFLIGHT now requires both onground_all = 0
  AND ground speed >= 20 m/s. Prevents false liftoff triggers at
  terrain-complex airports (e.g. LFHU) where the aircraft can momentarily
  read "not on ground" while still rolling.
- Reverted: The v1.4.4 gear/y_agl detection attempt (sim/flightmodel2/gear/y_agl
  does not exist in this X-Plane build). Returns to onground_all-based
  landing detection.
- Note: This is the build shared with Gamma.
