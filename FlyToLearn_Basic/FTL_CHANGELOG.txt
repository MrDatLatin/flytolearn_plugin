----------------------------------------------------------
FlyToLearn Basic 1.3.4 — 2026-04-26
----------------------------------------------------------
- Fixed: Plugin state machine froze in "Waiting to depart"
  because sim/flightmodel2/gear/y_agl does not exist in this
  X-Plane build. Switched to sim/flightmodel/position/y_agl
  (aircraft body AGL) and capture an on-ground baseline at
  Start click, so detection auto-adapts to any aircraft.
- Liftoff threshold: body must rise 1.0m above on-ground
  baseline to confirm INFLIGHT.
- Touchdown pre-capture window: body within 0.5m of baseline
  while descending.
- LANDED transition: body within 0.2m of baseline (~wheel contact).

----------------------------------------------------------
FlyToLearn Basic 1.3.3 — 2026-04-26
----------------------------------------------------------
- Fixed: "Landed on Wrong Runway" disqualification when touchdown
  was actually inside the safe zone. Touchdown position is now
  recorded at the actual moment of wheel contact, not at the
  point where the plane slowed to ~45 kts.
- Fixed: False "Enroute" banner during takeoff roll at ~45 kts.
  Phase transition now uses gear-height (sim/flightmodel2/gear/y_agl)
  instead of the unreliable onground_all weight-on-wheels sensor.
- Added: Per-gear height pre-capture using vh_ind_fpm2
  (instantaneous vertical speed) for accurate landing scoring.

FlyToLearn Basic 1.3.2
----------------------------------------------------------
- Added landing quality scoring: G-force penalties and disqualification
- Added runway zone check (touchdown_lat/lon vs safe landing zone)
- Added wrong-runway detection (upper half of runway = wrong direction)
